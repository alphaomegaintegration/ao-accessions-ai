The problem

The delay doesn't live in the forms. It lives in the back-and-forth.

In 2020 a recruit could enlist in under a week. Today it's routinely 30–45 days — and the time is lost in medical reviews, waivers, and document chase cycles, while policy changes constantly. Applicants drift and are lost during the wait. Forms aren't the bottleneck; the iterative request-wait-resubmit loop is.

Every loop costs days

Each "we also need X" from the government is another round trip with the applicant — days of dead time, repeated for each missing document.

Policy never sits still

Required documents per medical factor change frequently. Hard-coded processes go stale; the work becomes re-training and rework.

Minimal AI today

Current tooling is largely manual and legacy. The opportunity for AO is to compress the loop with governed automation.

The model

One connected, instrumented case for the cadet's whole journey.

Everything becomes a single Cadet Case with child Document Requests, a Medical Factor, and SF-86 status. Capture once, reuse everywhere — no re-keying — so automation has a clean spine to run on and every cycle is measured.

How it works

Hybrid AI: native where licensed, external agent everywhere else.

The same outcome — documents, AI case notes, recruiter tasks — produced in any edition. That means the demo runs identically and clean every time, and the path to a fully licensed Agentforce org is a swap, not a rebuild.

Native automation

Built & running in the org
  • Record-triggered Flow on Cadet Case save
  • Predicted Document Requests created automatically
  • Status sync across the case
  • A factor or form change = a config/table edit, minutes not a dev cycle

External agent

Identical outcome, any edition
  • Completes the full policy-driven document checklist
  • Writes the AI case summary + drafted applicant request (Einstein replacement)
  • Queues the recruiter follow-up task (admin agent)
  • Idempotent — never duplicates what the native Flow seeded

Licensed AI layer

Drop-in when Agentforce is on
  • Einstein prompt templates for case summaries
  • Agentforce Accessions Assistant
  • Same orchestration — agents just become native
  • Scales headless on a schedule for new cadets

Trust & governance

AI surfaces, drafts, and automates. Humans decide.

Consistent with SEAD 4 and accessions policy, AI never makes an eligibility, selection, suitability, or clearance determination. It removes the wait, not the judgment.

AI does

Predicts and creates the document checklist, drafts the applicant request, summarizes the case, queues the recruiter task, syncs status, and counts cycle time.

Human does

Approves every applicant send, makes every eligibility / suitability / selection / clearance decision, and signs off before anything leaves the org.

Built-in controls

Idempotent automation (no duplicate or runaway actions), IL4 hosting 1.11.1.59, PII/PHI handling 1.11.1.75, full activity audit trail.

Adapt safely

New policy = a reviewed config/table change. The decision rules stay visible and governed — no opaque model deciding outcomes.
