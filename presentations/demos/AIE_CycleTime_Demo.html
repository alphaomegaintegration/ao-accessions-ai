Executive summary

The problem isn't recruiting candidates. It's getting them through accession — fast.

Salesforce and AIE modernized the recruiting workflow, but the longest delays now hit during medical screening, waivers, and documentation collection. And because policy changes constantly, adaptability matters more than optimizing one static process. This demo shows how Alpha Omega attacks cycle time and policy agility — with "Army Pays for College" as just one example of a broader accessions message.
